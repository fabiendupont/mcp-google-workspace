{{- if .Values.networkPolicy.enabled }}

A NetworkPolicy restricts ingress to the configured client peers and egress to
DNS plus TCP/443 to the public internet. It is enforced by the CNI, not by
Kubernetes: on a CNI without NetworkPolicy support it has no effect. Verify your
CNI before relying on it, and note that `kubectl port-forward` does not go
through the policy.
{{- end }}

Prometheus Operator CRDs.
